finding your own funding for a PhD

These programmes fund PhD students every year. If you think one of these fits you, please be in touch with me well before the deadline because most need a supervisor’s support when you apply.

  • College of Science and Engineering Graduate School Scholarship (University of Glasgow). Covers full tuition fees and a stipend at the UKRI rate, in any research area of the College. You need a supervisor’s support before you apply. Applications are due by 31 January. [details]
  • DiveIn Centre for Doctoral Training (EPSRC, University of Glasgow). A fully funded, four-year interdisciplinary PhD programme. Students join a cohort without a set project, and form their project and supervisory team during the programme. Our work fits best with its Planetary Health research area. [details]
  • IAPETUS and ExaGEO (NERC doctoral training programmes). Both run a studentship competition every year, with new projects posted each autumn. [IAPETUS] | [ExaGEO]
  • James McCune Smith PhD Scholarships (University of Glasgow). For Black UK-domiciled students, in any discipline. [details]
  • China Scholarship Council scholarships.

I also welcome MSc by Research students.

The best way to get in touch with me to explore potential PhD funding routes is through this expression of interest form.
