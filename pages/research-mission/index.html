/ xander huggins' research website

Our mission is to use interdisciplinary, data-driven approaches to study groundwater and freshwater as parts of social-ecological and human-Earth systems with the goal of generating process-based insights to support physical groundwater sustainability, ecological integrity, social well-being and justice, inclusive development, and Earth system resilience through improved water management, policy, advocacy, and education.

Reflexive and good research practices are at the core of how we work. We are committed to open science: we share our preprints, data, and code. We also take part in workshops and seminars to learn tools and approaches that reduce bias and promote equity in higher education, and we put them into practice, for example by including positionality statements when we share resources.

current research directions

  • groundwater-dependent ecosystems
  • groundwater sustainability
  • freshwater social ecology and socio-hydrogeology
  • multidimensional development and human-Earth system dynamics

our research themes

We organise our research around four core themes:


Theory development on groundwater and freshwater as social-ecological systems. [ex1]


Data-driven characterisation of human-Earth systems, from groundwater systems to multidimensional development. [ex1/ex2]


Process-based insights on groundwater interactions within social-ecological and Earth systems. [ex1/ex2]


Connecting theories of change across social-ecological and human-Earth systems to support sustainability transformations. [ex1]
