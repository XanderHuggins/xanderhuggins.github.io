/ xander huggins' research website

Xander is an Assistant Professor (UK Lecturer) in Global Terrestrial Water in the School of Geographical and Earth Sciences at the university of glasgow. He is also an Affiliate Lecturer in the James Watt School of Engineering.

He is a visiting research collaborator at the High Meadows Environmental Institute at princeton university, and an associate with the stockholm resilience centre.
